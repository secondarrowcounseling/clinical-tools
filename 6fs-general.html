The System Spotlight

Your internal system uses protective parts to manage metabolic load. When a part reacts with high intensity, it is trying to prevent a system collapse. Use this control board to isolate a specific part. We will gather data on how it operates without getting swept up in its energy.

Step 1

Find (The Radar Ping)

Locate the specific physical signal in your body. Notice where the energy spikes, feels heavy, or creates tension. Click the radar grid below to mark the location.

Click to drop a pin.

Step 2

Focus (The Dimmer Switch)

Direct your attention bandwidth entirely toward this specific part. Use the slider below to visually dim the rest of your internal network. Do not try to change the part. Just watch it run.

Step 3

Flesh Out (Data Collection)

Observe the mechanical output. Use the mixing board to log the sensory data of this protective part.

Step 4

Feel Toward (The Anchor Check)

Check your starting point. Move the gauge to reflect how you currently feel toward this part.

Urgent / Annoyed Curious / Anchored

This urgency is an adaptive response. Ask this new protector if it can lower its intensity so we can look at the original target.

Step 5

Befriend (The Debrief)

Acknowledge the work this part is doing. Validate the massive metabolic cost required to keep this specific strategy running. This is an adaptive response, not a problem behavior.



Step 6

Fear (The Threat Assessment)

Identify the core mechanism. What specific system collapse or sensory flooding is this part actively trying to prevent?
