The System Spotlight

Your internal system uses protective parts to manage metabolic load. When a part reacts with high intensity, it is trying to prevent a system collapse. Use this control board to isolate a specific part. We will gather data on how it operates without getting swept up in its energy.

Step 1

Find (The Radar Ping)

Locate the specific physical signal in your body. Notice where the energy spikes, feels heavy, or creates tension. This moves the part from an abstract thought to a measurable reality.

Step 2

Focus (The Dimmer Switch)

Direct your attention bandwidth entirely toward this specific part. Visually dim the rest of your internal network. Do not try to change the part. Just watch it run.

Step 3

Flesh Out (Data Collection)

Observe the mechanical output. What does this part look or sound like? What is its current energy drain on your battery?

Step 4

Feel Toward (The Anchor Check)

Check your starting point. Do you feel urgency, annoyance, or frustration toward this part? If yes, another protective part is driving. Pause until you return to a neutral anchor.

Step 5

Biefriend (The Debrief)

Acknowledge the work this part is doing. Validate the massive metabolic cost required to keep this specific strategy running. This is an adaptive response, not a problem behavior.

Step 6

Fear (The Threat Assessment)

Identify the core mechanism. What specific system collapse or sensory flooding is this part actively trying to prevent?
